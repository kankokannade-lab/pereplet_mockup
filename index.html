The expedition vessel dropped anchor just as twilight enveloped the rocky bay. The crew gathered on the upper deck to inspect the nautical charts under lantern light. The wind had ceased almost entirely, leaving only the steady swell of the northern tides murmuring against the hull.

He folded the navigation chart carefully, knowing that any mistake along the reef could cost them days. Captain Somov then stepped toward the railing and ordered the landing boats into the water.

The sailors worked in complete silence, aware of the heavy surf pounding against the dark cliffs ahead. Within minutes the oars hit the freezing water, pulling away toward the silhouette of the old navigational tower.

Nobody spoke of what had happened during the previous survey. The official report was locked away in the archives of the admiralty, yet every man in the boat knew the legends that surrounded this godforsaken fjord.
